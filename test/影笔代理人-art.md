## S01 一心堂画室

设计意图：老街二楼的老式画室，既是沈砚的工作空间，也是影契发生的神圣/诡异场所。空间要旧、要安静、要有时间沉淀感。

### 一致性锚点

1. **木格窗** — 半开，夕阳透进来在地板投下格子光
2. **画架速写本** — 笔尖悬在纸面上方，墨痕在黄昏光下泛冷光
3. **老旧木地板** — 有岁月划痕和蜡油痕迹，沈砚站立处空无影子
4. **松烟墨味** — 视觉上通过墨瓶、砚台、笔筒呈现

### 光照与时段

- **黄昏**：`Interior of old Chinese artist studio on second floor of a heritage street building. Warm orange-grey dusk light streaming through half-open wooden lattice window. Dust particles floating in light beam. Ink bottle and brush pot on wooden desk. Old wooden floor with visible grain and wax marks. No people. Cinematic warm tones, shallow depth of field.`
- **路灯亮起**：`Same interior studio but dusk has faded. Yellow streetlamp light through wooden lattice window casting grid shadows on old wooden floor. Studio interior dim, only desk lamp and ink bottle catching faint glow. No people. Cool blue ambient mixed with warm yellow window light. Quiet, eerie atmosphere.`

## P01 速写本与墨痕

戏剧功能：缔约工具——照心纹、代行纹、偿价纹三道纹路在速写本上生成。沈砚右手的墨痕是身份标记，结尾异常发烫。

### 一致性锚点

1. **厚皮速写本** — 翻开在新的一页，三道纹路生成时纸面嘶嘶响
2. **右手墨痕** — 沈砚手背上淡墨痕，像烙印，黄昏时泛冷光
3. **纸边碳化** — 契约完成时纸边卷起碳化，飘下半透明影屑

### 状态变体

- **空白待画**：`Blank thick-sketch sketchbook opened to a fresh page, lying on wooden desk. Black ink bottle and fine brush beside it. Pure white background, no hands, no people. Product photography style, soft studio lighting. Handheld size reference.`
- **三道纹路成型**：`Sketchbook page with three intersecting ink patterns spreading like ink dropped in water. Delicate flowing lines forming abstract runes. Paper edges slightly curling and carbonizing. Pure white background, no hands, no people. Macro shot, dramatic lighting. Handheld size reference.`
