## S01 设计院第三会议室

设计意图：长条形会议室，日光灯，十二个人围坐，主位在一端。要的是职场的压抑感和当众打脸的戏剧性。

### 一致性锚点

1. **长条形会议桌** — 坐了十二个人，桌面摊着项目进度表
2. **主位** — 主管坐的位置，居高临下
3. **日光灯** — 冷白色，照得每个人脸都很白
4. **文件夹证据** — 周明放在桌上的一叠纸

### 光照与时段

- **日光灯上午**：`Interior of modern corporate conference room. Long rectangular table seating 12 people. Cold white fluorescent ceiling lights. Head of table on the far end. Window blinds half closed. No people. Realistic office style, cold tones.`

### 出图提示词包

**主视角 EN**

```text
Empty modern corporate conference room, long rectangular table, 12 office chairs around it. Cold white fluorescent lighting. Project schedule papers on table. No people, no shadows. Wide establishing shot.
```

**反向提示词**

```text
people, person, human, face, hands, character, animated, cartoon, anime
```

**设定图 EN**

```text
Single scene reference sheet. Wide shot of conference room interior. Shows long table, chairs, fluorescent lights, head of table position. Cold tones.
```

`conference room`, `office interior`, `fluorescent light`, `cold tones`

## S02 一心堂画室

设计意图：复用第1集画室场景，本集是上午阳光直射，与黄昏形成对比。

### 一致性锚点

1. **木格窗** — 上午阳光直直照进来
2. **画架速写本** — 沈砚在速写本上记远程感知
3. **老旧木地板** — 上午阳光照亮地板，沈砚脚下依旧没有影子

### 光照与时段

- **上午阳光**：`Same artist studio interior but morning sunlight streaming directly through wooden lattice window. Bright warm light on old wooden floor. Easel and sketchbook on desk. No people. Warm golden morning light.`

## P01 离职申请与证据文件

戏剧功能：周明当众揭露的物证——11个项目图纸、147封邮件记录、离职申请。

### 一致性锚点

1. **一叠纸** — 放在会议桌上，最上面是离职申请
2. **文件夹** — 灰色文件夹，周明攥在手里

### 状态变体

- **放在桌上**：`A stack of papers on a conference room table, top sheet is a resignation letter. Black ink documents. Pure white background, no hands, no people. Desktop size product shot.`
