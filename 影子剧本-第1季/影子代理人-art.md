## S01 一心堂画室

设计意图：这是沈砚的静室，也是全剧所有契约发生的地方。要空、要旧、要松烟墨味，木地板中央永远留着一块没有影子的亮。

### 一致性锚点

1. **木格窗** — 老式木格窗半开，黄昏光一格一格铺在地板上
2. **画架与速写本** — 靠窗立着旧木画架，台上摊开速写本
3. **对面木椅** — 委托人坐的那把旧木椅，椅腿磨亮
4. **右手干净处** — 沈砚站立的位置，地板上永远没有影子
5. **墙上炭笔速写** — 墙上贴着一排委托人背影小像

### 光照与时段

- **黄昏暖光**：`warm orange dusk light filtering through wooden lattice window, long striped shadows on old wooden floor, dim interior`
- **正午冷光**：`bright cool midday sunlight streaming through lattice window, harsh white squares on floor`
- **深夜路灯**：`very dark interior, only a thin strip of yellow street-lamp light from the window, charcoal dust floating`

### 出图提示词包

**主视角 EN**

```text
Empty interior of an old Chinese second-floor studio on a narrow old town street. Wooden lattice window half open, a worn wooden easel with a sketchbook, a wooden chair opposite, charcoal sketches taped on wall, old wooden floor with a clean patch where no shadow falls. Dusk warm light through window. No people, no hands.
```

**反向提示词**

```text
people, human figure, hands, modern furniture, bright daylight, new renovation, clutter
```

**设定图 EN**

```text
Environment setting sheet on ONE 16:9 landscape canvas. LEFT ZONE about 60%: wide establishing view of the studio interior in dusk light, lattice window left, easel center, chair right. RIGHT ZONE about 40%: four small detail studies stacked vertically — 1) wooden lattice window close-up, 2) sketchbook pages, 3) worn wooden chair, 4) charcoal sketches on wall. Surfaces: old matte wood, aged paper, chalk-dusted canvas.
```

`empty interior`, `old Chinese studio`, `dusk light`, `wooden floor`, `ink-wash atmosphere`

## P05 沈砚的旧怀表

3. **掌心焐热** — 沈砚贴肉揣着，用体温焐
