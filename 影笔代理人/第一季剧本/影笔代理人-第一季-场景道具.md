## S01 一心堂画室

设计意图：代理人的据点与工作台——老街二楼临街小画室，黄昏橘光从木格窗斜进来，墨味裹着旧纸味，是接单、画影契、藏秘密的唯一暖处。

### 一致性锚点

1. **半开木格窗** — 二楼临街木格窗，糊纸泛黄，半开对着老街屋檐
2. **窗光画架** — 旧松木画架立在窗光里，夹着半开的速写本
3. **月牙墨砚** — 长条旧木桌上一方黑砚，墨池边缘磨出月牙痕
4. **卷角速写墙** — 墙面钉满炭笔速写，纸角卷起发黄
5. **光柱浮尘** — 斜射光束里浮着墨色细尘

### 光照与时段

- **黄昏**：`low orange sunset light slanting through the half-open wooden lattice window, long warm shafts across the worn wooden floorboards, dust motes glowing in the beams, deep amber shadows gathering in the corners`
- **夜戏**：`only a small oil lamp on the long wooden table, a tight warm pool of light around the easel, the rest of the room falling into deep shadow, no street light through the paper window`

### 出图提示词包

**主视角 EN**

```text
empty scene, no people, second-floor old Chinese street-side painting studio, a weathered pine easel standing in slanting orange sunset light through a half-open wooden lattice window on the left, a long worn wooden table with a black ink stone and an open sketchbook, walls covered with pinned charcoal sketches with curled yellowed edges, dust motes glowing in the light shafts, worn floorboards, warm amber atmosphere, no people, no hands
```

**反向提示词**


